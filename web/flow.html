RESEARCH DESK
What the US options market is positioned for on each of your names, read from the official public record of open interest in US listed options, updated once a day after the close (-day expiry window). The names are the US lines on Desk · Book and the names on Watch · US; a name added below goes on Watch · US and is read within a minute. Biggest positions are the single contracts with the most open interest. OI builds compare open interest against the previous daily snapshot on disk, so they appear from a name's second covered day. Describes positioning only; it makes no call.
Reading open interest… a full sweep takes about a minute.
Put/call = open interest in puts divided by open interest in calls, across the window. Walls = the strikes with the most open interest in the window; dealer hedging tends to pin near big walls into expiry. Open interest counts contracts still open at the last close, so it moves once a day.
